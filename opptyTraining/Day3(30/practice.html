About

Shivaji Maharaj was born on February 19, 1630,
at Shivneri Fort near Junnar in present-day Maharashtra to Shahaji Bhonsle
, a Maratha general, and Jijabai, a deeply religious and influential mother who instilled in him values of courage,
justice, and devotion to the motherland. From a young age,
he was trained in military tactics, administration, and governance under the guidance of Dadoji Konddev and his mother.
At the age of 16, he captured Torna Fort,
marking the beginning of his campaign to establish an independent Maratha kingdom
